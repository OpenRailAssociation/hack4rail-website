# Route Roulette – A Playful Allocation Quest

## Problem Statement

The Swiss rail network is politically expected to continue growing, while financial resources remain limited. Future investment decisions require conscious prioritisation, because every expansion project creates additional longterm costs for maintenance and renewal. 

Existing approaches (e.g., simple allocation keys or manual simulation games) have proven insufficient, overly timeconsuming, or not accepted by stakeholders.

## Proposed Solution

Develop an interactive simulation tool ("Route Roulette") that enables users to allocate resources within a defined network perimeter and instantly visualise the consequences.

The outcome should enable wellfounded, factbased decisions under resource constraints.

## Required Expert Profiles

- Usability & visualisation experts 
- Data scientists & simulation specialists 
- Business analysts and corporate developers 
- Infrastructure managers and railway asset experts
