# Who Makes the Rules?

## Problem Statement

Legal, technical, and business regulations must be translated into concrete operational and IT rules. This translation is mostly manual, difficult to trace, and hard to maintain as regulations change over time.

## Proposed Solution

An AI‑supported approach to extract, structure, and manage business rules from regulatory and technical documents. The result is a consistent, traceable, and machine‑readable rule base usable across systems and processes.

## Required Expert Profiles

- Compliance and regulatory experts 
- Business analysts and process experts 
- AI / LLM engineers 
- Railway domain specialists
