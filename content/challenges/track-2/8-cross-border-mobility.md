# Build an Open Model of Cross‑Border Mobility

## Problem Statement

Cross‑border travel demand is poorly understood and remains car‑centric. Open, reusable mobility models are largely missing.

## Proposed Solution

Use open data to model cross‑border mobility and demonstrate rail‑based alternatives. The model serves as a reusable foundation for planning and policy analysis.

## Required Expert Profiles

* Mobility and transport modelers
* Data scientists
* Python and open‑data specialists
