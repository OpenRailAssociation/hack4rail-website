# One Screen. One Journey. Integrated Station Information incl. Rail Replacement

## Problem Statement
Indoor navigation in railway stations usually depends on costly positioning infrastructure. This limits scalability and prevents widespread deployment across stations.

## Proposed Solution
Develop a landmark‑based AI wayfinding solution using structured station data and spatial reasoning. Navigation works without indoor positioning, relying on semantics and topology instead.

## Required Expert Profiles

- Station data experts and data scientists 
- AI engineers and routing specialists 
- Customer experience and UX experts
